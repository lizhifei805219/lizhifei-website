关于我们

Professional Supplier of Precipitated Calcium Carbonate

Shijiazhuang Shaofei Trading Co., Ltd.

石家庄邵飞商贸有限公司，专业从事轻质碳酸钙（PCC）、活性碳酸钙的出口销售。依托源头工厂，产品广泛应用于塑料、涂料、橡胶、造纸等行业，专注服务东南亚市场（越南、菲律宾、泰国、马来西亚、印度尼西亚）。

核心产品 HB-101 超细沉淀轻质碳酸钙，执行标准 HG/T 2226-2019，品质稳定，白度≥92°，CaCO₃纯度≥98%，深受国内外客户信赖。

常见问题

解答您的疑问，促进合作信任

What is the MOQ? 最小起订量是多少？

+

MOQ is 20 MT (1×20GP FCL) for regular orders. Sample orders are also available for first-time customers to test product quality.

What is the lead time? 交货周期是多久？

+

15-20 working days after deposit confirmation. Regular stock can be shipped within 7 days. Custom orders may vary.

What payment terms are accepted? 支持哪些付款方式？

+

30% T/T deposit + 70% balance before shipment. L/C at sight is also negotiable for long-term cooperation.

Can we get free samples? 可以提供免费样品吗？

+

Yes, free samples are available. You only need to bear the shipping cost. Samples are dispatched within 1-2 days.

How is product quality guaranteed? 产品质量如何保证？

+

Each batch is tested in our lab with full COA (Certificate of Analysis). All test items comply with HG/T 2226-2019 standard. Third-party inspection is also accepted.

Do you support custom packaging? 支持定制包装吗？

+

Yes. Standard packing is 25kg PP woven bag with PE inner liner. Custom packaging with your brand logo is available for bulk orders.

What is the price term? 价格条款是什么？

+

USD 180.00 / MT FOB Tianjin Port, China. Other trade terms (CIF, CFR, etc.) are also available upon negotiation.
